    Hello!

    As a multi-disciplinary designer, I am passionate about creating meaningful experiences that connect people to products through design. With over 29 years of professional experience in various design fields, including illustration/fine art, branding/logotype design, structural/packaging design, interaction/user experience design, and end-to-end product design, I have developed a diverse skill set that allows me to approach projects from multiple angles.
